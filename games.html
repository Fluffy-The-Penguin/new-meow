Game modes

What each mode actually does.

Trivia and riddles

Players answer questions before time runs out. Trivia can be multiple-choice or typed-answer, while riddles focus on short clues and exact guesses.

/trivia/riddle

Word games

Wordle, Hangman, Word Chain, and Scramble test vocabulary in different ways: guessing hidden words, continuing chains, or solving shuffled letters.

/wordle/hangman

Pokemon and countries

Guess from silhouettes, flags, capitals, clues, or population prompts. These rounds work well when a channel wants quick answers from many people.

/wtp/countrygame

Coin games

Blackjack, dice, roulette, slots, coin flips, PvP challenges, and lotteries use coins as stakes or rewards, so wins affect player progression.

/blackjack/gamble

Social games

Truth or Dare, Never Have I Ever, Paranoia, Would You Rather, ships, hugs, and anonymous prompts are conversation starters for casual servers.

/truthordare/wouldyourather

Pet battles and events

Pets can fight other pets, join tournaments, appear in profile systems, and connect with timed XP or coin events that give players shared goals.

/petfight/tournament
